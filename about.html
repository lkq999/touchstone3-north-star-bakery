About North Star Bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: make dependable, carefully baked food for the people who live and work nearby. What started as a small bread counter grew into a neighborhood bakery where regulars stop for breakfast, families pick up weekend treats, and local event planners request cakes and pastry trays.

How we source

We focus on straightforward ingredients and choose regional suppliers when practical. Flour, dairy, fruit, and seasonal flavors are selected for quality and consistency so each batch tastes familiar while still reflecting the season.
